Convenience, Mystique and Unsupported Authority

A Conceptual Framework for Evidence-Sensitive Appraisal of Claims

How do convenience, narrative appeal and asserted authority influence the way we check a claim? This working paper proposes the CMF framework and a programme of tests to find out whether it improves decisions beyond existing approaches.
